About ROOTS

Research beyond the syllabus.

ROOTS stands for Research Opportunities and Outlook Talks for Students.

ROOTS is an undergraduate mathematics seminar at Virginia Tech that introduces students to research and developments in higher mathematics through accessible, engaging talks.

ROOTS welcomes students of all backgrounds and experience levels and is committed to fostering an inclusive environment.

Students from mathematics, CMDA, computer science, engineering, and related fields are welcome. Speakers may include faculty, graduate students, professionals in industry, and undergraduates themselves.
