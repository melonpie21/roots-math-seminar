About ROOTS 01

Mathematics beyond the syllabus.

ROOTS is a student-centered seminar created to expose undergraduates to research and happenings in higher mathematics at an accessible level.

The seminar welcomes students from mathematics, CMDA, computer science, engineering, and any other field where curiosity leads to mathematical questions. Talks may come from faculty, graduate students, professionals in industry, or undergraduates themselves.
